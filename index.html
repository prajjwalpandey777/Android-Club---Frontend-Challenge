Learn by building. Grow together. Ship real projects.

Android Club VIT Bhopal is the official technical club for mobile and software development at VIT Bhopal University. We bring passionate developers together to learn, build and innovate, from Android and Kotlin to Flutter, React Native, IoT and ML integrations.

Whether you are a beginner or a seasoned developer, there is a place for you here.

Vision
A campus where every student can turn an idea into a working product.
Mission
Teach through hands-on projects, workshops and hackathons, and build in the open.
Culture
Collaboration first: pair up, review each other's code, share what you learn.

What we do

Android development

Kotlin, Java and Jetpack Compose, from first app to shipped project.

Web development

React, Next.js and modern JavaScript, including our open-source DocsLab.

Research and development

IoT and ML integrations, like Android Fusion and our Edge AI prototype.

UI/UX design

Material Design and Figma: interfaces that are clear and a pleasure to use.

Projects

Real repositories from our GitHub organization.
